Last Updated: August 2026

To provide our research services and manage subscriptions, we collect personal information, which may include:

  • Identity Information (Name, PAN, DOB)
  • Contact Information (Email, Phone, Address)

We use your information strictly for the purpose of providing research analyst services, which include:

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Subscriber data is stored securely on encrypted servers. 4. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share necessary data with third-party service providers (such as payment processors or compliance auditors) solely for the purpose of facilitating your subscription or meeting regulatory requirements. All third parties are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, review, and request correction of your personal data held by us. You may also withdraw your consent for data processing at any time, subject to legal or regulatory restrictions which may impact our ability to continue providing research services.
